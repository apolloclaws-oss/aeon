# Agent Displacement Tracker

*Last run: 2026-05-25*

## Known Displacement Events (baseline)
- Klarna (2024): replaced 700 customer support agents with AI. Support resolution time 2min vs 11min human avg.
- Duolingo (2024): cut ~10% of contractors, cited AI content generation replacing human translators.
- Salesforce (2025): froze non-essential hiring across sales/support, citing AI agent handle rate.
- IBM (2024): paused hiring ~7,800 back-office roles that AI could replace within 5 years.

### Updates / new events (2026)
- **Meta (2026-05-20):** ~8,000 cut (~10% of ~78,865) plus ~6,000 cancelled reqs → effective ~14,000 reduction. Explicit "AI-first" reorg; ~7,000 staff redeployed into new AI groups (Applied AI Engineering, Agent Transformation Accelerator, Central Analytics). Framing: small teams + AI agents replace large departments. (CNBC, TechJournal, May 18–20.)
- **Salesforce (2026 update to 2025 baseline):** Benioff says customer-support headcount cut ~9,000 → ~5,000 (≈4,000 roles) with Agentforce now handling ~50% of customer interactions. Recency of the exact figure is soft (circulated via podcast/X); treat as standing signal, re-verify.
- **Goldman Sachs × Anthropic (2026):** deploying autonomous Claude agents for trade accounting and client onboarding — first clear breach into finance back-office ops. No headcount disclosed yet.
- **Coinbase (2026-05):** ~700 cut (14%), CEO Armstrong framing as shift to "smaller, AI-augmented teams."
- **PayPal (2026-05):** ~4,760 (~20% of 23,800) over 2–3 yrs, CEO citing intent to "accelerate AI adoption and automation."
- **Cisco (2026-05-14):** ~4,000 cut. **Oracle (2026 Q1–Q2):** up to ~30,000 (~20%). **Cloudflare (2026-05):** ~1,100 (~20% of 5,156); internal AI usage +600% in 3 months. (Context; most predate the May 18–25 window.)

### Reversals / counter-signals (track for thesis)
- **Klarna (2026 reversal):** rehiring human agents under a hybrid model after AI-only support dropped quality/CSAT and pulled engineers onto tickets. The original cautionary tale on over-automating tier-1.
- **Gartner (2026-02):** predicts 50% of firms that attributed customer-service cuts to AI will rehire (often under new titles) by 2027; Oct-2025 survey found only 20% of CS leaders had actually reduced staff. "AI washing" widely flagged (Oxford Economics, Altman).

## Roles Under Pressure (running list)
- Customer support / tier-1 help desk (Salesforce, Klarna — but see Klarna reversal)
- Content translation and localization
- Technical writing / documentation
- Data entry and document processing
- Code / dev: junior developers, QA testers, code review
- Legal document review (discovery)
- Finance back-office: trade accounting, client onboarding (Goldman × Anthropic)
- Entry-level / new-grad pipeline broadly (hiring freezes more than mass firing)

## Displacement Scale Estimates
- **2026 YTD (to 2026-05-18):** 113,863 tech layoffs across 179 events; one tracker attributes ~48% to AI (heavily debated — "AI washing").
- 2025: 54,836 layoffs explicitly attributed to AI by employers; modeling estimates put actual AI-displaced/foregone roles at 200K–300K.
- Stanford: employment among early-career workers in AI-exposed occupations down ~16% since ChatGPT (2022).
- 2024 baseline: ~2M white-collar roles affected (McKinsey / Goldman estimates).
- Mechanism shifting from layoffs → hiring freezes + higher output-per-employee; entry-level pipeline narrowing fastest.
